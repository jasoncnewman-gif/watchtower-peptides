## 4. Top 3 Recommended Vendors

**Superpower (10/10)** — Only vendor with both IGF-1 and explicit peptide/prescription access alongside the most complete standard-panel biomarker coverage (359 items) and disclosed CLIA certificate numbers — directly relevant to a peptide-researcher audience. Strongest direct fit for a peptide-researcher audience given explicit peptide access messaging; note the NY/NJ surcharge and at-home draw fee are real added costs worth flagging for budget-conscious readers.

**Marek Diagnostics (10/10)** — OYO/a-la-carte model (not a flat consumer subscription) and its tight coupling to a TRT/peptide telehealth parent company makes it a different buying pattern than mainstream at-home test kits, even though its biomarker depth and peptide-researcher relevance are the strongest of any vendor in the whole audit. Best peptide-researcher fit of any vendor: only vendor with Amylase, Lipase, and Zinc as literal standalone orderable tests, plus IGF-1, full hormone panel, and a direct pipeline into peptide/TRT prescribing via Marek Health. No HOMA-IR or NAD+ as sellable products.

**Vitals Vault (9/10)** — Largest raw catalog (~1,400+ individually indexed a la carte tests, corroborated by independent PR coverage), no-subscription one-time-purchase model is a genuine differentiator, and Max tier covers nearly the full peptide-critical marker set including IGF-1 — main caveats are default thyroid panel uses older T3-Uptake methodology rather than Free T3/T4, and company funding/track record is unverifiable. Strong fit for researchers who want a-la-carte flexibility to build custom panels around specific peptide biomarkers without committing to a subscription; the T3-Uptake-vs-Free-T3/T4 default is worth flagging explicitly.

## 6. Excluded Vendors

- **MyHealthspan**: Strongest raw biomarker panel among comparable vendors (full liver/kidney-partial/hormone/lipid coverage, venous draw, real clinical review) but Canada-only availability — confirmed still true, with no US expansion beyond stated intent to build HIPAA compliance for a future launch — makes it inapplicable to Watchtower Peptides' primarily US audience. Corrected from a prior INCLUDE-with-caveat call: geographic inaccessibility is treated as disqualifying, consistent with how other excluded vendors were assessed.
- **TruDiagnostic**: TruHealth is a methylation/metabolomics nutrition panel, not a clinical hormone/organ-function panel. Despite marketing '105+ biomarkers,' it contains zero of the hormone markers (testosterone, IGF-1, LH/FSH, cortisol) and zero of the liver/kidney safety markers that peptide researchers monitoring GH-secretagogues or hormone-modulating peptides actually need. Its real strength (NAD+ metabolite tracking, biological age via TruAge) is a genuine but narrow niche unrelated to the core peptide-research use case. Reclassified from INCLUDE-NICHE to EXCLUDE now that the full marker list is confirmed (rather than mostly unconfirmed) and shows zero overlap with core safety/hormone monitoring.
- **Ways2Well**: Confirmed telehealth prescribing clinic requiring a clinician consultation/relationship, not a direct-to-consumer self-service blood testing platform. Peptides and hormones are dispensed by prescription following a paid consult rather than sold as an OYO lab-test product, which places Ways2Well outside the comparison set's scope. Deeper research reconfirms rather than overturns the prior conclusion.
- **Just Labs**: The task's original target (justlabs.com) is a dead/parked domain unrelated to any operating business. The actual live entity at justlabs.health is a straightforward self-pay OYO Quest lab marketplace with strong biomarker coverage relevant to peptide researchers, but it is a pure diagnostics/test-ordering service with no peptide/hormone therapeutic angle, no clinician interpretation, and no distinguishing longevity-platform features (no biological age score, no AI health coaching, no trend tracking) that would justify inclusion over the comparison set's target profile of purpose-built longevity/blood-testing platforms.
