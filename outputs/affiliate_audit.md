Two categories are tracked separately per vendor where applicable: **creator/content affiliate programs** (what would apply to a Watchtower content deal) and **customer referral programs** (member give/get perks — usually not usable for content monetization).

## Customer referral programs (separate from above, generally not usable for content affiliate deals)

- **Superpower**: none found beyond the $25 program itself (which is structured as an open affiliate program, not a customer perk)
- **Vitals Vault**: none found
- **Function Health**: $25 to both referrer and new member, no cap, excludes healthcare professionals
- **Hundred Health**: "Give $25, get $50" — low confidence, sourced only from a third-party review, not confirmed on an official page
- **InsideTracker**: exists but reward figure conflicts across sources (20% off + free test vs. 35% off both sides)
- **Everlywell**: $35 gift card to referrer + 15% off for referred friend, $600/year cap — solidly confirmed, live at refer.everlywell.com
- **OneTwenty**: not separate — one unified fixed-fee structure covers both customers and creators
- **Mito Health**: $50 credit when referred friend completes their lab test (not just purchases)

## Key flags before using any of this in content or a vendor comparison table

1. **Only 2 of 8 vendors (Superpower, Vitals Vault) have commission terms confirmed with high confidence.** The rest are either undisclosed, gated behind application, or conflicting across third-party sources.
2. **Superpower's figures were previously claimed as "already confirmed" without ever being verified — they turned out to be accurate.** Vitals Vault's "10% commission" claim was directionally right but imprecise (it's a ceiling, "up to 10%," not a flat rate).
3. **OneTwenty and Function Health's referral program use fixed-fee/non-percentage structures for regulatory reasons** (both offer prescription-adjacent services) — a "commission %" column won't apply cleanly to them in a comparison table.
4. **Mito Health has no public affiliate program at all** — third-party cashback aggregators (StackWell, Refermate) exist but are independent services with self-reported, conflicting rates, not Mito Health's actual terms.
5. **Do not conflate customer referral programs with content-affiliate programs** — several vendors (Function Health, OneTwenty in particular) explicitly structure these as legally distinct programs with different eligibility rules.
6. **Where conflicts exist** (InsideTracker's cookie window, Everlywell's commission rate), the only way to get a single authoritative number is to actually apply to the relevant network and view live program terms — not accessible via public research.
